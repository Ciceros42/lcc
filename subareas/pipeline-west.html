A short walk west of the pipeline brings you to a more isolated cluster of boulders with the widest grade range in the canyon. Jug Haul is the easiest problem in the area; the Overhang Project is one of the hardest. The Compression Problem is the standout line — a unique style of movement not found anywhere else in LCC.

Jug Haul

V0 ★☆☆
Jug Haul — topo photo coming soon

Massive, bucket jugs up a vertical wall. Exactly what it sounds like. The easiest problem at Pipeline and a good choice if you brought a friend who has never bouldered before.

West Face Direct

V3 ★★☆
West Face Direct — topo photo coming soon

A direct line up the center of the west face on varied, high-quality holds. The movement shifts from crimps to sidepulls to a sloper finish — great for practicing movement transitions. A solid mid-session problem.

Compression Problem

V7 ★★★
Compression Problem — topo photo coming soon

Squeeze a rounded arête using opposing body compression — no positive holds, just friction and core tension. The movement is unlike anything else in LCC. Taller climbers have a slight advantage but technique trumps height here. A unique and coveted tick.

Overhang Project

V9 ★★☆
Overhang Project — topo photo coming soon

A powerful roof problem requiring serious upper body strength and contact strength. The first move off the lip is the hardest — a massive lock-off to a tiny edge. Low send percentage but very rewarding. Get strong and come back.
