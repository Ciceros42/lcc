The first boulders you reach at the Pipeline area, sitting right at the pipeline itself. The rock here is slightly more featured than Pipeline West with a mix of corner cracks, crimp faces, and one crowd-pleasing dyno. A good area for mid-grade climbers looking for quality problems away from the busy parking lot.

Classic Corner

V1 ★☆☆
Classic Corner — topo photo coming soon

Follow the obvious corner crack up the east face of the main Pipeline boulder. Solid crack technique gets you to a ledge finish. A good warm-up for the harder problems nearby and a reliable tick for beginners.

The Ramp

V2 ★★☆
The Ramp — topo photo coming soon

An angled ramp leads to a tricky exit sequence on the upper wall. Easier than it looks — once you spot the right foot sequence, the whole problem unlocks. Deceptively fun and often soloed without pads by regulars.

Pipeline Crimp

V4 ★★★
Pipeline Crimp — topo photo coming soon

An elegant line of small, positive crimps on a 45-degree wall. The movement is continuous but never desperate — each hold is good enough to pause on if you stay in balance. One of the best moderate problems at Pipeline.

The Dyno

V5 ★★☆
The Dyno — topo photo coming soon

A crowd-pleasing all-out jump from a low side-pull to a high sloper. You either hit it or you don't — there's no halfway. Fun to try at the end of a session. The dyno itself is straightforward once you find the right body position at the jump.

Sit Down Special

V6 ★★☆
Sit Down Special — topo photo coming soon

The sit-start to Pipeline Crimp adds two brutal low moves before rejoining the regular sequence. The initial move off the ground is the hardest — a long compression reach to a sloping sidepull. Worth projecting if you're comfortable on V4.
