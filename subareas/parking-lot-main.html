Main Wall

Parking Lot Boulders

Problems5
Grade RangeV0–V5
Rock TypeGranite
AspectSouth / East
SeasonSpring & Fall
The Main Wall is the central cluster of boulders at the Parking Lot area, visible directly from the road. The rock is polished from heavy traffic but still provides solid friction. Excellent for warm-ups and introductory problems — regulars often start every session here regardless of their project grade.

Massive jugs up the east face. Simple movement but great for warming up cold fingers before the harder problems. Kids love this one.

Granite Kiss

V1 ★★☆
Granite Kiss — topo photo coming soon

Follow a left-trending rail system on perfectly featured granite to a ledge finish. One of the best warm-up problems in the canyon — the rock quality is exceptional and the movement flows naturally.

The Egg

V3 ★★★
The Egg — topo photo coming soon

Start low on a pair of smooth underclings and work up a rounded, egg-shaped face to a committing sloper top-out. The crux is the final reach — trust your feet and stay calm. A LCC classic that sees traffic every weekend.

Mantle Shelf

V4 ★★☆
Mantle Shelf — topo photo coming soon

Jump to a high ledge and press out over the lip in a full mantle. The initial jump is committing and the press-out demands shoulder strength. A great problem for building mantle technique on bomber granite.

Slopers Anonymous

V5 ★★★
Slopers Anonymous — topo photo coming soon

A long traverse from right to left across the Main Wall on a series of increasingly worse slopers. Sustained and surprisingly pumpy for a boulder problem. Best conditions in cool, dry weather when friction is maxed.
