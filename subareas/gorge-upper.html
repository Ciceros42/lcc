The upper portion of the gorge opens slightly, exposing a cluster of taller boulders with more complex movement. Crimson Tide is the benchmark hard problem of the entire canyon. The Prow offers one of the most aesthetic lines in LCC. Stay shaded most of the day.

Upper Slab

V3 ★★☆
Upper Slab — topo photo coming soon

Sustained low-angle slab climbing with a distinct crux high step near the top. The high step feels bold but the landing is flat. A good step up from Slab Master in the lower gorge for climbers developing slab technique.

The Prow

V5 ★★★
The Prow — topo photo coming soon

The striking arête that juts out at the top of the gorge. Balance and precision on the exposed prow of a large boulder — holds are sparse and the position is airy. One of the most photogenic lines in the canyon.

Shadow Line

V6 ★★☆
Shadow Line — topo photo coming soon

In the shade most of the day, making it a good option during warm afternoons. Technical crimping sequence on a slightly overhanging face with a sloper finish that catches everyone off guard. Go when conditions are good — it's friction-dependent.

Crimson Tide

V8 ★★★
Crimson Tide — topo photo coming soon

The crown jewel of The Gorge. A sustained sequence of dynamic moves on crimps leads through a powerful lock-off crux at the top. Every single move is hard and the sequence is unforgiving. One of the best hard problems in LCC bouldering.
