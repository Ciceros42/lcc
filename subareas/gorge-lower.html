The entrance to the gorge is marked by two large boulders that nearly touch overhead. The Lower Gorge boulders are well-shaded by the canyon walls and stay cooler than the parking lot area. The diverse problem set includes slab, power, dihedral, and one of the longest traverses in the canyon.

Boulder Hopper

V1 ★☆☆
Boulder Hopper — topo photo coming soon

A stepping problem across two adjacent boulders with a short face sequence in the middle. Straightforward movement but worth doing for the position inside the gorge. Good intro for new boulderers.

Slab Master

V2 ★★☆
Slab Master — topo photo coming soon

Technical footwork on a perfectly angled 70-degree slab. Every hold is an edge and every move demands precise smearing. This problem teaches you more about climbing than 50 jug hauls. Wear clean, soft shoes.

The Dihedral

V3 ★★☆
The Dihedral — topo photo coming soon

Classic stemming problem in a tight granite corner. Use opposing pressure on both walls to walk your way up. The technique is everything — brute force won't get you to the top. A great introduction to dihedral movement.

Gorge Traverse

V5 ★★★
Gorge Traverse — topo photo coming soon

A long traverse following a horizontal crack system through the lower gorge. Pumpy and committing as the holds deteriorate toward the right end. Stay low on the crack and use heel hooks to rest when you can. One of the longer problems in LCC.

Power Trip

V7 ★★★
Power Trip — topo photo coming soon

Starts low in a sit-start from matching on a flat edge just above the ground. Two explosive moves lead to a sloper rail — then you have to hold it. High-output, low-count movement on impeccable granite. A Gorge benchmark problem.
