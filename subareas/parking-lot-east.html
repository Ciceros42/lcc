Two minutes east of the Main Wall, the East Side boulders sit in a slightly more secluded position on a dirt bench above the road. The problems here are harder and less polished — featured, coarser granite with sharper holds. The tall east face is home to Highball Heaven, which sees fewer ascents due to the commitment required.

Crimper's Delight

V4 ★★★
Crimper's Delight — topo photo coming soon

A stunning line of sharp, positive crimps on a slightly overhanging face. The crux sequence near the top involves a long reach between two small edges. Often cited as one of the best V4s in the entire canyon.

Pinch Fest

V4 ★★☆
Pinch Fest — topo photo coming soon

Consecutive pinch moves on a steep 45-degree overhang. Your forearms will be burning by the third pinch. A great trainer for anyone working on pinch strength — unusual movement for this area of the canyon.

Highball Heaven

V6 ★★☆
Highball Heaven — topo photo coming soon

The tallest problem on the East Side. A committing sequence of crimps up a vertical face leads to a scary top-out on sloping holds 18 feet up. Pad stacks are highly recommended. The movement is actually moderate — it's the height that's the challenge.

Slap Happy

V7 ★★☆
Slap Happy — topo photo coming soon

A dynamic slap from a side-pull to a huge feature on the upper wall, followed by a tenuous sequence to the top. The slap itself is the crux — you either stick it or you don't. Very satisfying when it all comes together.
