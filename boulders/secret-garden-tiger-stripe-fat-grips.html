Fat Grips V9 ★★★

Start on a left crescent edge rail and a right sidepull then move up under the overhang and punch right to the lip

Big Paw V8 ★★★

Start on Fat Grips and finish on Bored Stiff

Fat Camp V9 ★★★

Start on Fat Grips, but finish up the arete to the left rather than punching right

Morbidly Obese Grips V10 ★★★

Start matched on left arete then climb right into Fat Grips

Bored Stiff V5 ★★★

Originally started matched on the left arete, but is usually started now with a left on the arete and right on the crescent edge. Either way it climbs straight up over the bulge onto the slab above

Weight Watchers V10 ★★★

Start matched on the left hold of Bored Stiff and climb right finishing up the arete

Fatter Grips V10 ★★☆

Start on underclings down low and climb up into Fat Grips

Fatter Camper V10 ☆☆☆

Do the low sit start the same as Fatter Grips , but climb into fat camp.

Biggest Paws V10 ☆☆☆

Sit start the same as Fatter Grips with dual underclings. Climb up and left into big paw. For a harder challenge eliminate the jug hold on fat grips.
