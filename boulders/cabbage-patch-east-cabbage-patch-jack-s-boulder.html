Fat Albert Gang V3 ★★★

Well loved barn door arete with a nice landing - start standing with hands matched on the arete at the top of the cyrstal seam and climb up the arete to the point

Jack's Slab V2 ★★★

Climb slabby features over a subtle bulge - great problem with a flat landing

Fungus V4 ★★★

Start sitting against the arete with left higher on the crimp pinch and a lower right sidepull then pull on and hit a juggy edge up and right and finish up over the lip

Fat Albert Gang Sit V4 ★★★

Start sitting with hands matched on the arete and climb up into Fat Albert Gang

Chipped Arete V1 ★★★

Climb the quarried arete on the right side, good lines, shelf near the ground makes the landing a little weird

Mono Move V4 ★★★

Start on crimps below the good rail, then hit the rail and punch to the good jug rail above, finish by finding the hiddend mono above and right and using it to pull over the lip

Jack V1 ★★★

A Bit Slopey V7 ★★★

Christopher Reeve V2 ★★★

Jack's Slab Left V2 ★★★
