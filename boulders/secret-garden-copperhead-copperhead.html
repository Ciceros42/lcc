Twisted V4 ★★★

Start matched on good left facing holds in the middle of the rail and twist your way up and right through the dihedral

Slab 2 V0 ★★★

Start standing on the shelf and climb the slab

Bear Hug Sit V8 ★★★

Start sitting on the notch and sloper and climb into Bear Hug

Backdoor Bear Hug V11 ★★★

Start on the far left side of the sloper rail and slap traverse right into Bear Hug. A variation has been done that starts further left on the sidepull jug which makes it a little harder.

Slab 1 V0 ★★★

Nice edges on excellent rock, start with hands high and foot on the jug

Slab 3 V0 ★★★

Start standing on the left side of the shelf then climb out to the arete and up

Slab 2 Sit V2 ★★★

Sit start on the shelf and climb into Slab 2

Breaking Dawn V10 ★★★

Start right on the arete and left on a sidepull crimp then head up and left to the point

Slimliminate V2 ★★★

Pull on with slim sidepulls and climb between slab 1 and slab 2 avoid the good holds on either side

Not Copperhead V9 ★★★

Start same as Copperhead but after hitting the crimp punch all the way out to the arete then up

Funky Bunch V10 ★★★

Start sitting with a notch sloper and climb straight up the overhanging face

Slab 0.5 V1 ★★★

Start with high hands on slim crimps and climb the slab

Funky Bunch Stand V9 ★★★

Start with a notch on the left end of the big sloper rail and go straigh up the overhanging face

Slab 1 Sit V2 ★★★

Jonny’s Hug V9 ★★★

Start standing two moves in from Backdoor, same as Johnny's Mantle, then climb right into Bear Hug.

Ap's Eliminate V4 ★★☆

Start sitting and mantle onto the shelf without using any other holds above it, then finish on Slab 2

No Name V4 ★★★

Backdoor Bunch V13 ★★★

Manly Arete Sit V8 ★★★

Mr. Mom V10 ★★★

Contrail V6 ★★★

Lance's Left V8 ★★★
