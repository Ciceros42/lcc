The Shield V4 ★★★

Start on two right facing holds in the middle of the face, then climb up to the shelf, and left through the notch to top out

Shield Direct V8 ★★★

Start on the rounded rail features, then climb straight up onto the higher rail and move up to the notch to top out

The Shield SDS V8 ★★★

Start sitting and matched on the low left corner of the boulder. Move up/right through the crimp and then the big under cling until you can link into The Shield and top out in the notch.

Shield Low V6 ★★★

Start on the rounded rail features to the right of The Shield, then traverse left and finish on The Shield

Shield Right V8 ★★★

Start on small edges to the right of the rail, then traverse left into The Shield

Shield Face V7 ★★★

Start matched on the same edge as Shield Arete then climb straight up

Shield Right Exit V8 ☆☆☆

Shield Left V7 ★★★

Shield Arete V5 ★★★
