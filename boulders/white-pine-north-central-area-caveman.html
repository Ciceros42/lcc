Neolithic V10 ★★★

The sit start to Caveman. Start at the bottom of the pit with left hand on flat edge and right hand on good low side pull. High tension side pulls and a technical heel hook lead to better holds until you reach the rail and begin the traverse that is Caveman V4ish. Follow the rail all the way out right and top out on jugs.

Caveman Low V8 ★★★

This is the original low to Caveman after Blake and Austin had cleared out the cave. Starts with left heel on the starting left hand of Neolithic. Left hand on rounded crimp and right hand on low right rounded grip. Neolithic adds the two lower start moves.

Bone Club V9 ★★★

This is the arete line on the Caveman boulder. Starts deep at the bottom with a low left hand jug and the large flat ledge on the right side of the arete.
