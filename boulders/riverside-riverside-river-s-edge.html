Jim V4 ★★★

Classic intermediate with a variety of memorable moves - start low with good right arete sidepull and left crimp rail sidepull then move up the arete and traverse back left to reach the arete and pull the crux to get establish on the slab above. Start variations have been done, can start low on edges around the arete to the right and finish on Jim
