Condemned V8 ★★★

Sit start with left hand in the slot crimp, right hand gaston edge, and a toehook out right. Climb straight up through horizontal slots to the sketchy jug rail. Topout on choss, or drop off.

The Generous Three V5 ☆☆☆

Around the corner to the right of Condemned in a corridor. Start with a left hand on the arete and right hand on a sidepull on the face. Climb straight up.
